PARENTS OF RUYTON

The Parents of Ruyton (POR) is the umbrella organisation for all of the parent support groups in the School. The POR engages our parent community in social activities and strengthens the bond and interaction between parents and the School.

OLD RUYTONIANS' ASSOCIATION

Our alumnae are known as Old Ruytonians and includes any student who has attended Ruyton for all or part of their education. An active Alumnae community through events and activities conducted by The Old Ruytonians' Association (ORA).
